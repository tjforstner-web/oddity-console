What is Oddity Console?

Oddity Console is the dashboard and transparency hub for Compile Oddity. It is a website for status, story, and disclosure. It is not a token.

What is the overall project called?

Compile Oddity, the parent project. Its line is “Different by design.” Oddity Console is the dashboard and transparency hub for Compile Oddity.

What are the three identities?

StillComp ($STILLCOMP) is The Process, DoublePenny ($DBLPENNY) is The Growth, and CHOMP ($CHOMP) is The Watchdog. They are told through two X accounts, @WIPOperator and @DBLPENNY.

Has $STILLCOMP launched?

No. $STILLCOMP has not launched, and no contract exists. Any mint you see with that name or ticker is not from this project unless a mint is later announced from @WIPOperator.

What is the penny-doubling experiment? Does it track the price?

It is a 30-day fictional doubling experiment around $DBLPENNY. The story amount on Day N is $0.01 × 2^(N−1), starting November 18, 2026 (ET), and stopping at Day 30. Future chapters are undecided. The counter has nothing to do with the token’s price. It is a story device, not a record of price performance.
