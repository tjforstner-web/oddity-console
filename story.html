The Story

How the three identities of Compile Oddity connect. Oddity Console is the dashboard and transparency hub where this is written down.

StillComp came first. DoublePenny was a practice run. Getting rugged inspired CHOMP. The same creator built all three.

That origin was stated publicly on @WIPOperator. This page does not add details about what “getting rugged” referred to, because that has not been specified here.

Three identities, two accounts

  • StillComp ($STILLCOMP) — The Process. The original concept. A tribute to people who are still compiling. Not launched. No contract exists. The process lives on @WIPOperator.
  • DoublePenny ($DBLPENNY) — The Growth. A 30-day fictional doubling experiment, told on @DBLPENNY. Future chapters are undecided. The counter is a story device. It has nothing to do with the token’s price.
  • CHOMP ($CHOMP) — The Watchdog. A symbolic watchdog, not a security or monitoring service. A dev rugging holders is stealing. CHOMP does not scan, audit or protect anything, and does not vouch for any token. There is no separate CHOMP account; the character appears on the two accounts above.

What Oddity Console is

Compile Oddity is the parent project. Its line is “Different by design.” Oddity Console is the dashboard and transparency hub for Compile Oddity. The line on this hub is “Three identities. Two accounts. One evolving story.” It is where status, story, and disclosure are meant to live in one place. It is not itself a token.

What this is not

This is a meme project with an interconnected story. There are no promises about price, returns, or volume. Meme coins have no utility. You may lose your entire purchase. Read the Disclosure page for the factual record, including truncated wallets and the second-wallet trading disclosure.
